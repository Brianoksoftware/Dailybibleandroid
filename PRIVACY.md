# Privacy Policy — Daily Bible Friend Offline

**Last updated: 26 September 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains what information Daily Bible Friend Offline (“the App”) handles, how that information is used, and what choices you have. Daily Bible Friend Offline is a Bible verse app. It does not require an account.

## 1. Information we collect

Daily Bible Friend Offline does not require you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, password, or location.

We do not operate our own servers that store your verses, bookmarks, or reading list. We do not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs.

## 2. Information stored on your device

The App stores a small amount of data on your device so its features work. This may include:

- Bookmarked verses (reference, text, and related details)
- Reading-list items if you unlock Pro
- How many free searches you have used today
- Your light or dark theme preference
- Whether Pro has been unlocked on this device

This information stays on the device. We do not receive a copy of it. You can remove bookmarks and reading-list items in the App. Deleting the App removes this locally stored data, subject to how your device normally works.

## 3. Purchases

If you buy Bible Friend Pro, the purchase is processed by Google through Google Play Billing. We do not collect, see, or store your card number, Google account password, or other payment details.

Google may handle information needed to complete the purchase, prevent fraud, and restore what you bought. That handling is governed by Google’s privacy policy.

The App only stores a local record that Pro is unlocked on this device so unlimited search and the reading list keep working. You can restore a previous purchase with the Restore Purchases control. Restoring uses Google Play purchase records, not an account in Daily Bible Friend Offline.

## 4. Information received by third-party Bible services

Some verses are bundled in the App. When you browse more passages, open a verse for surrounding context, or run a live search, the App may request text from public Bible services, including bible-api.com and bolls.life.

Those requests can include the passage or search terms you asked for, plus ordinary technical information such as your IP address and the time of the request. We do not send your bookmarks, reading list, or Pro status to those services.

bible-api.com and bolls.life operate their own servers and decide how they handle information. Daily Bible Friend Offline does not control their practices. Please review their policies:

- [bible-api.com](https://bible-api.com/)
- [bolls.life](https://bolls.life/)

## 5. Sharing

If you use Share, your device opens the system share sheet. You choose where the verse goes. Daily Bible Friend Offline does not receive a copy of what you share unless you separately send it to us (for example by email). The app or service you share with has its own privacy policy.

## 6. Information we do not collect

Daily Bible Friend Offline does not ask for or directly collect:

- Name, email, or phone number (except if you choose to email us)
- Precise location beyond what ad networks may infer
- Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
- Payment card details

Free users may see banner ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect device identifiers, IP address, and approximate location to show and measure ads. That handling is governed by Google’s privacy policy. Pro removes ads. We do not sell personal information for our own advertising.

## 7. Advertising

If you use the free App, we show Google AdMob banner ads on some screens (such as Home and Search). Ads are not shown after you unlock Bible Friend Pro.

Google may show a consent message where required (for example in the EEA/UK). You can change privacy choices through that form when it is available. We do not place ads over verse reading content or use surprise full-screen ads.

## 8. Internet access

The daily verse and many popular passages can appear from text stored in the App. An internet connection is used for additional verses, surrounding context, live search, and ads.

If the App is offline, features that need the network may not work. Your internet provider and the Bible services you reach may process technical information such as your IP address under their own policies.

## 9. Children

Daily Bible Friend Offline is a general-audience Scripture app. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Because the App does not require an account and does not collect personal information from users, we do not keep a database of children’s information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Retention and deletion

We do not keep a server-side copy of your bookmarks, search history, or reading list.

You can remove bookmarks and reading-list items in the App. Search counts reset each local calendar day. Deleting the App removes locally stored App data, subject to your operating system.

Google retains purchase and ad records according to Google’s policies. Information handled by bible-api.com or bolls.life follows those services’ own retention practices.

## 11. Security

We limit what the App handles to what its features need. Data stored on your device also depends on your device’s security. No method of storage or internet transmission is completely secure.

## 12. Changes

We may update this policy if the App’s features, data practices, or third-party services change. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date. Please review this policy from time to time.
